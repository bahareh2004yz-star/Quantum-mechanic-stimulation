# Quantum-mechanic-stimulation

# Numerical Methods in Physics

This repository is a collection of my projects and exercises in computational physics, mainly focused on exploring quantum mechanics using Python.

Through these projects, I have been working with numerical methods to solve physical problems, calculate quantum states, and visualize their properties.

## Projects

**1. Particle in a Box**

An exploration of the one-dimensional infinite potential well, including the calculation and visualization of wavefunctions, probability densities, and energy levels.

**2. Free Particle**

A numerical study of the one-dimensional Schrödinger equation for a free particle, focusing on its energy states and corresponding wavefunctions.

**3. Harmonic Oscillator**

A numerical investigation of the quantum harmonic oscillator, using matrix diagonalization to calculate its energy eigenvalues and eigenfunctions.

## Tools & Libraries

- Python
- NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook


The main goal of these projects was to improve my understanding of computational methods in physics and gain more experience in programming.
